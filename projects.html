A few experiments and write‑ups from my ML/LLM tinkering.

Quick Draw Diffusion

What it is: A compact diffusion training pipeline built around HuggingFace UNet2DModel, trained on five categories from Google QuickDraw (banana, apple, broom, broccoli, birthday cake). Each class is sampled (~1k sketches), normalized to 32×32 with padding, and combined with a class‑indicator channel so the model learns class‑conditioned noise prediction across timesteps.

How it works: A noise scheduler controls timesteps; the model predicts noise and is optimized with MSE. Adam with a cosine schedule is used; utilities cover dataset IO, batching, and sampling to visualize learning.

Stylistic LLM Fine Tuning

What it is: Notes and experiments in nudging an LLM’s style while preserving factuality and conversational ability. The initial run uses TinyLlama‑1.1B‑Chat‑v1.0 on karpathy/tiny_shakespeare with QLoRA, adapting a small fraction of parameters to keep the footprint modest.

Next Steps: Longer training, alternative adapters, and comparing prompt‑tuning vs. LoRA for controllability. Also developing a good evaluation technique to compare the finetuned vs base model.
